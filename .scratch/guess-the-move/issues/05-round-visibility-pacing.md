## Question

Can Participants see each other's submissions before Reveal, and — since speed pressure is explicitly unwanted — how does a Round end and advance?

## Answer

**Visibility**: fully hidden. No Participant sees another's Guess or Analysis Tree, and the Host Display shows none of them either, until Reveal. The Host Display only shows who has/hasn't submitted, never what. This also matters because the Host-person may be a Participant on a separate device (see [Host dual role & display parity](../issues/09-host-dual-role-display-parity.md)) — anything the Host Display leaked early would be something that Host-as-Participant could exploit.

**Pacing**: Host-paced, manual advance. The Host watches submissions come in and triggers Reveal when ready, then manually advances to the next Round. This replaces Kahoot's timer/speed-race mechanic with presenter control, matching the "reward correctness and thought, not speed" goal.

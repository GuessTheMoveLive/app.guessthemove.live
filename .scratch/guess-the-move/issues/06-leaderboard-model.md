## Question

How do Round Scores combine into the Session's Leaderboard, and what's shown at each Reveal?

## Answer

A simple running total of Round Scores per Participant, shown after every Reveal, plus a **Momentum Highlight**: a callout of whichever Participant had the largest Round Score gain in the Round just revealed. This keeps the board feeling dynamic move-to-move rather than static once someone builds an early lead, without introducing a separate scoring mechanic (streaks, decay, etc.) beyond the running total.

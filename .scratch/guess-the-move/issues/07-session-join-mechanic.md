## Question

How do Participants find and join a live Session on their phones?

## Answer

Room Code + URL, shown on the Host Display: a short code and an accompanying URL/QR code, mirroring the familiar Kahoot join pattern. Chosen over QR-only so there's a fallback for anyone who can't scan (projector glare, no camera access). No accounts needed to join.
